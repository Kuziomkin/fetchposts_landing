Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe to use with my LinkedIn account? expand_more

FetchPosts uses your own logged-in browser session and does not ask for your password. As with any automation tool, use responsibly and at your own risk. Your session cookies never leave your computer.

What data exactly is exported? expand_more

FetchPosts extracts posts with metrics such as views, likes, comments, reposts, and approximate dates into CSV — giving you a complete spreadsheet for analysis beyond what LinkedIn's UI provides.

Does it work on Windows? expand_more

Currently, FetchPosts is a native macOS application designed for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. A Windows version is on the roadmap but not available yet.

What happens if LinkedIn changes their layout? expand_more

We actively maintain the scraper. If LinkedIn updates their DOM structure, we push an update to FetchPosts to handle the changes.

Is there a refund policy? expand_more

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee if the software doesn't work on your machine as advertised.

Will this work with 2FA enabled? expand_more

Yes. Since FetchPosts uses your own logged-in browser session, you simply log in with your normal 2FA process, and the app can then access your authenticated session.

Will LinkedIn block me for using this? expand_more

We cannot guarantee this won't happen. FetchPosts automates browsing your own profile, similar to manually clicking through your posts. Use the tool responsibly, avoid excessive scraping in short periods, and understand you use it at your own risk.
