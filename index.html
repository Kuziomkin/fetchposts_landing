Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe? expand_more

Yes, it runs locally on your Mac using your active browser session. We never see your password or cookies.

What data do I get? expand_more

You get a CSV file with all your posts and key metrics: views, likes, comments, reposts, and dates.

Does it work on Windows? expand_more

Not yet. FetchPosts is currently macOS only (Intel & Apple Silicon). Windows support is coming soon.

What if LinkedIn changes? expand_more

We monitor LinkedIn daily. If they change their layout, we push an update to FetchPosts to fix it immediately.

Does it work with 2FA? expand_more

Yes. Just log in to LinkedIn normally with 2FA, and FetchPosts will use your active session.

Will I get banned? expand_more

Unlikely if used responsibly. The app mimics human browsing behavior. However, always avoid excessive scraping in short periods.

Can I switch computers? expand_more

Yes. Click "Deactivate" in the app on your old Mac, then activate the license key on your new one.
